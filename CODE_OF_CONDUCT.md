# Code of Conduct

## This is a joke

**Where's MC Graeballs?** is a joke website. **MC Graeballs is a character, not a real person.** The "sightings" are a game: they are not reports about a real person, and the site is not a way to track, find or follow anyone.

Any resemblance to a real person is coincidental. If you think a sighting, note or name refers to a real, identifiable person, it should not be there. Don't add it, and tell us so it can be hidden.

## What we don't condone

We do not condone harassment of anyone, for any reason, on or off this site. That includes:

- stalking, following, filming or tracking a real person, or posting where a real person is or was;
- doxxing, or sharing someone's personal information (names, addresses, phone numbers, photos);
- insults, threats, hate or discrimination aimed at anyone, including because of who they are;
- using the site, its notes, place names or nicknames to target a person or a group;
- sexual or otherwise unwelcome attention.

If the joke stops being funny for someone, it stops. Don't be weird.

## Using the site

- Keep notes, place names and spotter names clean and about the character only.
- Don't submit sightings you know are about a real person.
- Sightings that break these rules may be hidden or removed without notice, and rate limits and filters may block abuse.

## Contributing to this repository

The same standards apply to issues, pull requests, comments and any other space around this project. Be respectful and constructive. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove contributions that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly do.

## Reporting a problem

- **Content on the site** that targets a real person or breaks these rules: open a GitHub issue **without repeating the harmful details** (describe it, don't quote it), or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. It will be hidden as soon as it is seen.
- **Conduct in this repository:** report it to the maintainer via their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's "Report content" tools on the offending comment.
- **Security problems:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything urgent or involving a real threat to someone's safety:** contact the police (999 in the UK for an emergency, 101 otherwise). We are not a substitute for that.
